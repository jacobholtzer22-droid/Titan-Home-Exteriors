A single hung keeps the top sash fixed and slides the bottom one up, which is why it has the fewest moving parts and the lowest price of the styles we install.

## Is a single hung window enough for a Florida home?

For most openings, yes. The style does not decide how the window performs in heat or in a storm; the glass package does, and a single hung can be ordered with energy-efficient or impact-rated glass. Where a single hung struggles is reach: if the window sits above a sink or on a second storey, cleaning the outside of the fixed top sash means going outside with a ladder.
