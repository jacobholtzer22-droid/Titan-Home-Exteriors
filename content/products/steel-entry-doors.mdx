## Does a steel door rust this close to the coast?

A steel entry door is clad and finished, not bare metal, so what you are maintaining is the finish rather than the steel. Inland that is straightforward. Closer to the water, salt air is harder on any finish, and in those spots we will usually point you at fiberglass instead, which is a slower sell and the right answer.
