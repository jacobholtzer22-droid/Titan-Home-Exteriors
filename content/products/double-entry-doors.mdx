## Do I need a wider opening than I have?

Usually, yes. Going from a single door to a pair means widening the rough opening and carrying the load above it, which is work we scope at the measurement visit rather than guessing at from a photograph. The inactive leaf is held by flush bolts and an astragal, so day to day you use one door and open both when you want the full width.
