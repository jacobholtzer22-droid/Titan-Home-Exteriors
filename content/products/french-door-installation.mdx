## Can you replace a sliding glass door with French doors?

Yes, and it is one of the more common door jobs we do. We measure the opening on the estimate visit and quote the door and the installation together, because swapping a slider for a pair of swinging doors changes the framing, the threshold and the way the room is laid out around it. Swing direction is worth deciding on site rather than on paper.
