## Can a fiberglass door look like wood?

Most of them are moulded with a grain and finished to take a stain, and at a few steps back the difference is hard to pick. The practical gap shows up over years rather than at the showroom: a stained fiberglass slab holds its finish through Florida summers far better than a real wood door in the same opening.
