## Which siding material should I pick?

We install vinyl, fiber cement and engineered wood. Vinyl is the lowest cost and the lowest maintenance; fiber cement is heavier, holds paint well and handles sun and moisture; engineered wood sits between them and looks closest to real timber. Which one suits the house depends on its exposure and on how long you plan to own it.
