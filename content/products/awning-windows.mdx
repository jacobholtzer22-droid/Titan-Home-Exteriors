## Can I leave an awning window open in the rain?

In light rain, that is exactly what the style is for: the open sash sheds water away from the opening, so you can keep air moving through an afternoon storm. In a real storm, close it. Awning units are often placed high on a wall or above a fixed picture window, where a swinging sash is well out of the way.
