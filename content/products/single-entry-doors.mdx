## What makes an entry door last in Florida?

The slab material and the seal. Fiberglass handles heat and humidity without swelling or denting; steel resists impact and takes a lock well. Either way the door is only as good as the frame it hangs in, which is why we prep the opening plumb and fit the weatherstripping and sweep rather than treating them as an afterthought.
