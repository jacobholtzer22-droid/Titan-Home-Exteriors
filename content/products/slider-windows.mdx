## Why do old sliders get hard to open?

Almost always the track and the rollers rather than the sash. Grit works into the track, the rollers flatten, and the window starts needing two hands. We replace the unit rather than servicing the old one, because repair work is not something we take, and on a window that age the replacement is usually the cheaper answer over the next decade.
