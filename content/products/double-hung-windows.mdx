## Are double hung windows worth the extra cost?

It depends entirely on where the window is. Over a kitchen sink, on a second storey, or anywhere a ladder is a nuisance, the tilt-in sashes pay for themselves the first time you clean them. On a ground-floor window you can reach from the yard in ten seconds, the upgrade is harder to justify and we will say so.
