## How do I know whether I need impact glass?

It comes down to the wind zone, the size of the opening, and sometimes what your insurer wants to see. We work that out at the measurement visit rather than selling up by default. What we will say plainly is that upgrading later is not economical: if impact glass is on your mind at all, it is cheaper to do it once.
