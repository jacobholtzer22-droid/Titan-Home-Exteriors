## Does board and batten suit every house?

No, and that is worth saying before you order it. It sits naturally on newer builds and on anything leaning coastal or farmhouse. On a traditional ranch it can read as applied rather than original. A common middle path is battens on the gables or one accent elevation with lap siding across the body of the house.
