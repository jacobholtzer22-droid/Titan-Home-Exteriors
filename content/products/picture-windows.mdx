## Will a large window make the room hot?

The size of the pane matters less than the glass in it. We specify an insulated unit with a coating suited to the direction the window faces, which is the part that controls heat gain. Where a room needs airflow as well as light, we usually pair a picture window with an awning or casement unit on one or both sides.
