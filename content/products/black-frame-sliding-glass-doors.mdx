## Does a black frame get hotter in the sun?

A dark frame runs warmer than a white one in direct sun. Modern frames are built for it, and the heat that actually reaches the room is controlled by the glass package rather than the frame colour. If the opening faces west and gets full afternoon sun, that is a reason to talk about the glass, not to avoid the finish.
