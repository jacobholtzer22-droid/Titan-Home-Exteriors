{config.displayName} sells and installs new replacement windows for homeowners and commercial property owners in {config.primaryCity} and the surrounding counties, with the county permit pulled and the inspection booked by our own crew.

To be plain about scope: we install new windows. We do not repair windows and we do not replace broken glass, so if that is what the house needs, a repair company will serve you better. Window projects start at five windows.

## What window styles do you install?

Single hung, double hung, slider, picture, casement, and awning, in energy-efficient and impact-rated options. We represent twelve window and door brands, which means a spec can usually be met more than one way, and we will show you the trade-offs instead of steering you at one product.

## What happens after I sign?

The steps do not change. We come back for a final measurement, the order goes into production with materials rated for the Florida code, and you get an ETA. When the materials arrive we inspect them before they go anywhere near the house. Then comes installation day, the county inspection, and the final sign-off. After the inspection passes we install the caps, clean the panes, and walk the job with you.

## How long does a window project take?

We give you the timeline after the measurement visit, because it depends on what you ordered and when it comes out of production. What we will not do is invent a date to win the job.

## Do I need a permit?

Yes, and it is ours to handle. We pull the county permit and schedule the inspection as part of the project.

## Can I finance it?

Qualified buyers can finance through Momnt, a licensed consumer lending platform, with zero percent promotional offers available for a limited term. Financing is subject to credit approval by a third-party lender. Not all applicants will qualify. This is not a commitment to lend.
