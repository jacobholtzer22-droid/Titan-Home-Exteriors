## What makes a window impact-rated?

An impact-rated window is built and tested to hold together and stay in its opening when something strikes it. That is the whole point of the product: the opening stays closed, so the wind does not get inside the house. We install standard impact and HVHZ-rated glass, and every opening goes in to code.

## What does HVHZ mean?

High Velocity Hurricane Zone, the strictest wind rating in the Florida building code. We install HVHZ-rated windows and doors as well as standard impact products, so we can match whatever the project calls for rather than selling one answer to every house.

## Do impact windows replace storm shutters?

Impact-rated glass is designed to protect the opening on its own, which is why most people choose it over putting shutters up twice a season. On insurance, ask your insurer about wind mitigation credits. What your policy does is between you and them, and we will not promise you an outcome we do not control.
