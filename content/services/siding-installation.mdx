{config.displayName} installs and replaces siding in {config.primaryCity} and the surrounding counties, using the same crew that handles our window and door work. Windows and doors are the bulk of what we do, and siding is the fourth service on the list.

## What siding materials do you install?

Vinyl, fiber cement, and engineered wood, in lap board and board and batten styles. Which one suits the house depends on the exposure, the look you are after, and what is already on the wall. We go through the options on the estimate visit instead of handing you a brochure.

## Do you replace siding that is already there?

Yes. Siding replacement is part of the service, and because the same crew does our window installations, the detail where siding meets an opening is handled by people who fit windows for a living.

## What does the siding warranty cover?

A Lifetime Siding Limited Warranty covers the siding products, the installation workmanship, and labor on approved claims. The warranty certificate governs the full terms. Our [warranty page](/warranty) covers how the window and door coverage works alongside it.

## Should I do windows and siding at the same time?

If you are planning both, it is worth quoting them together. Windows and siding meet at the same openings, so one crew doing both is tidier than two jobs that have to hand off to each other.
