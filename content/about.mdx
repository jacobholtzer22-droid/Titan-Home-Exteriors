## Who we are

Titan Home Exteriors LLC was founded in Tampa with a simple idea: treat every home like it's your own. Show up on time. Return the phone call. Seal it right the first time. We focus on three services, windows, siding, and doors, so every project gets our full attention.

Each job is staffed by our own crew.

## Who runs the company

{config.displayName} is run by Michael Scalise, who comes from a family with three generations in construction. Behind the company sits more than twenty years in the window and door industry, which is why the measurement visit is done carefully and the quote is written rather than guessed at.

## Licensed, insured, and code-compliant

We are a licensed Florida contractor and we carry two million dollars in general liability coverage. Every opening goes in to code, the county permit is ours to pull, and the inspection is ours to schedule. After it passes we install the caps, clean the panes, and walk the finished job with you.

## What we install, and what we do not

We sell and install new windows and doors: replacement windows, impact-rated windows and doors, entry doors, French doors, and sliding glass doors. We also install siding. We do not take repair work, service calls, or glass-only jobs, and window projects start at five windows. Saying so up front saves everybody a wasted visit.
