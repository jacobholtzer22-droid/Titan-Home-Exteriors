## What the warranty covers

Our windows and doors carry a Double Lifetime Limited Warranty. It covers the products, the labor, the glass, and the installation workmanship for as long as you own your home. The warranty certificate governs the full terms, and we hand it to you with the paperwork.

- Product: all materials are covered against defects for as long as you own the home.
- Labor: one hundred percent of labor costs on every covered repair, with no service call fees.
- Workmanship: the installation is guaranteed for the lifetime of the original homeowner.
- Glass: seal failure and stress cracks are covered for life. See the warranty certificate for the full glass coverage terms.

## What it costs you when you make a claim

Nothing, on a covered repair. There are no deductibles, no trip charges, and no hidden fees.

## Does it transfer if we sell the house?

Yes, once. The warranty transfers to the next homeowner one time, which is worth knowing if you are putting windows in a house you may not keep forever.

## What is not covered

- Cosmetic damage from impact, misuse, or weathering.
- Acts of God, including hurricanes, floods, hail, and lightning.
- Unauthorized modifications or repairs by anyone other than Titan-authorized personnel.
- Improper maintenance.
- Pre-existing structural, rot, or moisture conditions outside the contracted scope of work.

## Siding warranty

Siding carries a Lifetime Siding Limited Warranty, covering the siding products, the installation workmanship, and labor on approved claims.

## The certificate is the document that governs

Everything on this page is a plain-language summary. The warranty certificate issued with your project is the document that governs coverage, so read it and keep it with your paperwork. Ask us for a copy before you sign if you want to read it first.
