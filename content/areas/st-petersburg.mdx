## Windows and doors in {area.name}

{area.name} sits on a peninsula, so two things are true at once: a lot of the housing stock is older than the suburbs inland, and the wind exposure near the water is higher. Bungalows and mid-century homes here often still carry their original windows, and the closer a house sits to open water the more sense impact-rated glass makes.

Older openings need careful measuring and sometimes a frame that is not a catalog size, which is part of why we measure before we quote.
