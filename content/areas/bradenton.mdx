## Windows and doors in {area.name}

{area.name} sits between the Manatee River and the Gulf, and it has a lot of older single-story homes alongside newer growth further east. Near the water the exposure argues for impact-rated windows and doors; inland, owners more often come to us about sliding glass doors that have given up.

Older homes here were built in eras with very different window standards, so what comes out tells us a lot about what should go back in.
