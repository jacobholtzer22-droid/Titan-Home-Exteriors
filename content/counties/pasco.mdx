Pasco has two distinct halves for our purposes. Along the SR 54 and SR 56 corridor through Wesley Chapel and Land O' Lakes, almost everything is master-planned and built from the 2000s onward. Out west through New Port Richey, Hudson and Holiday, the housing is older and much of it sits near the water.

## What does that mean for a window order?

On the newer side the openings are square and consistent, so a whole-house order moves quickly and the usual question is whether to upgrade the builder-grade units to impact-rated glass. On the older coastal side the openings need careful measuring and the exposure is higher, which changes both the glass package and the anchoring.

## What we install here

Window projects start at five windows. We sell and install new windows and doors only: no repair work, no service calls and no glass-only jobs. The estimate is free and it happens at the property, because the measurement is what an honest quote is built on.
