Pinellas sits on a peninsula between Tampa Bay and the Gulf, and it is the most densely populated county in Florida. The housing is older than the inland suburbs on average, with a lot of mid-century homes and bungalows in St. Petersburg and Clearwater and beach communities sitting directly on the Gulf.

## Why does impact glass come up so often here?

Because of where the water is. On the barrier islands and the beach communities the wind exposure is as high as anywhere we work, and the closer a house sits to open water the more sense impact-rated windows and doors make. Further inland the conversation shifts back toward energy efficiency and sliders that have stopped sliding.
