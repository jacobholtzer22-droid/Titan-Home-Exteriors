## Does an inland county change the specification?

It changes the emphasis. Orlando sits far enough inland that wind exposure is lower than it is anywhere on our coastal counties, so impact-rated glass is a considered upgrade rather than the default. Energy efficiency carries more weight here: west-facing glass in central Florida does real work on a cooling bill, and the glass package is where that is won.
