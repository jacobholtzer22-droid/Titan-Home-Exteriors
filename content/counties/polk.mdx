Polk sits inland between Tampa and Orlando, built around a chain of lakes, with Lakeland and Winter Haven as its largest cities. It holds some of the oldest housing stock we work on alongside some of the newest, with rapid growth around Davenport and Champions Gate on the Orlando side of the county.

## Is impact glass worth it this far inland?

It is a more open question here than it is on the coast. Inland Polk sees lower wind exposure than Pinellas or Manatee, so the choice between standard and impact-rated glass is genuinely a choice rather than a foregone conclusion. In the older Lakeland neighborhoods the bigger win is usually replacing single-pane wood and aluminum units that have been in service for decades.
