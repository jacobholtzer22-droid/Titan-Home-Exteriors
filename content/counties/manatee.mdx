Manatee runs from the barrier islands on the Gulf, through Bradenton and Palmetto on the Manatee River, east to Lakewood Ranch and Parrish. That is a wide range: older single-storey homes near the water, and some of the newest master-planned housing in the state further inland.

## How much does being near the Gulf change things?

On Anna Maria, Holmes Beach, Bradenton Beach and Longboat Key, the wind exposure is about as direct as it gets and impact-rated windows and doors are the normal specification. Inland around Lakewood Ranch and Parrish the exposure drops and the conversation is more often about sliding glass doors that have given up and about efficiency.

## What we install here

Window projects start at five windows. We sell and install new windows and doors only: no repair work, no service calls and no glass-only jobs. The estimate is free and it happens at the property, because the measurement is what an honest quote is built on.
