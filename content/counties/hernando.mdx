Hernando sits north of Pasco on the Nature Coast. Spring Hill is the largest community and most of it was built from the 1970s onward, which puts a lot of its original windows and sliding doors well past their service life. Brooksville, the county seat, is older and sits further inland.

## What do most Hernando jobs look like?

Whole-house replacement of original units, more often than not. On a house of that age the seals have usually failed on at least some of the windows, the sliders drag, and replacing them in one order costs less per opening than doing it in stages. Out toward Hernando Beach and Weeki Wachee the exposure is higher and impact-rated glass is worth pricing.

## What we install here

Window projects start at five windows. We sell and install new windows and doors only: no repair work, no service calls and no glass-only jobs. The estimate is free and it happens at the property, because the measurement is what an honest quote is built on.
