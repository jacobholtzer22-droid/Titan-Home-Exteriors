Hillsborough is where our office is, so it is the county we are in most days. It also has the widest spread of housing we work on: 1920s bungalows in the older Tampa neighborhoods, 1970s and 1980s subdivisions through Brandon and Carrollwood, and homes finished in the last few years across Riverview, Lithia and Apollo Beach.

## What changes across the county?

Age, mostly. In the older neighborhoods the openings are rarely square and the original aluminum frames are long past their service life, so measuring carefully matters more than anything else. In the newer subdivisions the openings are consistent and the job is usually a whole-house order of builder-grade units coming out at once. Along the bay the wind exposure argues for impact-rated glass.

## What we install here

Window projects start at five windows. We sell and install new windows and doors only: no repair work, no service calls and no glass-only jobs. The estimate is free and it happens at the property, because the measurement is what an honest quote is built on.
